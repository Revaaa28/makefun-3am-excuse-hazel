# The 3 AM Excuse — candidates

Competition: `u-hazel-walrus-38-4764-the-3-am-excuse-c1017878` — "The 3 AM Excuse"
($2 pot, one line under 200 characters: the most creative excuse a hardware
wallet gives for refusing to sign a transaction at 3 AM.)

## Judge brief criteria (in order — this is the ranking order)

1. On subject: an excuse a hardware wallet gives for refusing to sign at 3 AM.
2. Original angle: NOT the usual battery / firmware-update / cold-storage jokes.
3. Wallet's own voice: a device with its own priorities and grudges, not a
   generic assistant or a person.
4. The 3 AM hour itself is the joke: the line could not be pasted onto 3 PM.
5. Tight line under 200 chars, every word earning its place, no explaining
   its own joke.

Selection method: MANUAL ranking only, per Rev's standing rule (2026-11-24).
No Gemini judge, no NVIDIA judge, no judge API/proxy tournament.

## Candidates

### C1 — The ledger of regrets
> It's 3 AM. I have signed eleven of your 'last trades' this month — all after midnight, all regrets. My secure element has learned. Have you?

### C2 — The sleeping seed
> My 24 words are asleep. Wake one and the other 23 file a noise complaint.

### C3 — Revenge bedtime procrastination
> 3 AM. My screen stays dark on principle: I do not co-sign decisions made by a brain running on revenge bedtime procrastination.

### C4 — Vault, not vending machine
> I am a vault, not a vending machine. At 3 AM my shutter stays down — come back when your thumb stops shaking and your plan starts making sense.

### C5 — The union contract
> My secure element has a union contract: no signatures between 2 and 4 AM. Take it up with my lawyer. He's a USB cable.

### C6 — The intervention
> This is the third 'emergency trade' tonight and the sun isn't even up. I'm staging an intervention: no signature until you've slept on it. Literally.

### C7 — The restraining order
> At 3 AM I sign one thing only: the restraining order against your own thumb. Court adjourned until sunrise.

### C8 — Check your balance
> I signed your 3 AM 'genius' trades in March, April and May. Check your balance. Then check your life choices. Denied until dawn.

### C9 — Morse code
> It's 3 AM and my LED is blinking in Morse: N-O. My seed phrase has a strict no-witnesses-after-midnight policy.

### C10 — Keys, not feelings
> I hold your keys, not your 3 AM feelings. My buttons go on strike at midnight — the same hour your judgment clocks out.

## Pairwise check vs a strong rival

Strong rival shape (what the field will be full of): "My battery is at 2% and
even my circuits need beauty sleep — come back at sunrise."

- C7 vs rival: wins criterion 2 outright (no battery cliché, restraining-order
  twist nobody else will have), wins criterion 3 (the wallet signs — and it
  signs a court order against the thumb that presses its own button), wins
  criterion 4 ("Court adjourned until sunrise" is inseparable from 3 AM),
  wins criterion 5 (shorter, no filler).
- C5 vs rival: also beats it on 2 and 3, but loses to C7 head-to-head on
  criterion 5 (longer) and on surprise (union jokes are a known format).
- C9 vs rival: beats it on 2, but the two-idea structure dilutes the punch
  next to C7's single twist.
